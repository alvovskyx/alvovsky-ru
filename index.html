When there was no map

BIOCADClinical development · 2018, 2020

There was no template.

In 2018 nobody in Russia had written a clinical trial protocol for CAR-T cell therapy. In 2020, there was none for HER2 bispecific antibodies.

So I wrote the first ones. From scratch.

Notes. BIOCAD, Russia’s leading biotech company. The oncology portfolio: 14 trials, phases I–III, for the EAEU, EMA, FDA and other regulators.

Fertoing / Russian Marine TeamMarine robotics · 2025

The numbers didn’t agree.

A marine robotics company had to choose: build a 3,000 m underwater vehicle or a 400 m one. Public estimates of how many such vehicles work in Russia differed tenfold: from 50 to 700.

So I stopped choosing between estimates and built the registry: every operator and its vehicles, public tenders since 2020, customs declarations.

Market: ~₽7B in 2025, ~₽10B by 2030. The model went to the CEO and into the company’s reply to the Ministry of Industry and Trade.

Notes. Sources: EIS procurement 2020–2025, Federal Customs declarations, SPARK. The top-down estimate is cross-checked bottom-up.

GremediumBiotech, EU · Series A · 2022

The investors couldn’t read the technology.

A European biotech company was raising a Series A to scale up manufacturing. The science was sound. The investors couldn’t price its risk.

So I translated the risk into their language: the financial model, the due diligence, the investor conversations.

Round closed: €1.5M.

Leningrad RegionGovernment technology · 2021–2022

The answer took a month.

When an investor in the Leningrad Region asked a routine question, government bodies and utilities had up to 30 days to respond.

So we built the Investor’s Personal Account: the digital front door of the region’s investment agency, one route for every request.

Response time: 30 → 7 working days.

Notes. Public figure, reported by the regional government and Kommersant. The agency accompanies about 300 projects worth ~₽7 trillion.

Government of St PetersburgClient · 2022

There was nothing to measure with.

The St Petersburg government needed to know how digitally mature the city’s key enterprises were. No instrument existed.

So I built one: metrics, reporting, a transformation roadmap.

The directorate’s annual KPIs: exceeded by 20%.

BIOCADFederal funding · 2020

The samples sat unused.

A biobank of clinical trial samples: valuable in theory, useful to no one.

So I initiated a federal programme to sequence it: ₽30M of state funding.

It became a patent: a diagnostic tool that predicts which melanoma patients will respond to anti-PD-1 immunotherapy.

Notes. Patent RU 2771080 C2, 2022. Public record.
